## Response and scoring contract

Candidate instruction: «Lee cada frase y elige qué tan bien te describe en general. No hay respuestas correctas o incorrectas. Puedes cambiar tus respuestas antes de terminar.»

| Value | Candidate option |
|---|---|
| 1 | No me describe nada |
| 2 | Me describe poco |
| 3 | Me describe a medias |
| 4 | Me describe bastante |
| 5 | Me describe totalmente |

Labels chosen by the user (L17): «Me describe…» keeps the IPIP accuracy anchor (how well the statement describes the person), not frequency or agreement. Pilot comprehension before launch. Accept integers 1–5 only. Positive key: response; negative key: `6 − response`. Facet = sum of four scored values, range 4–20. Finish requires all 60 personality and 3 attention answers; no prorating, imputation or partial results. Attention never enters personality sums. No domain scores, personality total, rank, percentile or overall fit. Backend scores; keys and membership never reach the candidate. Fixed presentation order appears below and attention definitions in [quality signals](quality-signals-v1.md).
