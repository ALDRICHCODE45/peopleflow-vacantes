## Response and scoring contract

Candidate instruction: «Lee cada frase y elige qué tan bien te describe en general. No hay respuestas correctas o incorrectas. Puedes cambiar tus respuestas antes de terminar.»

| Value | Candidate option |
|---|---|
| 1 | Muy inexacto |
| 2 | Algo inexacto |
| 3 | Ni exacto ni inexacto |
| 4 | Algo exacto |
| 5 | Muy exacto |

Accuracy describes the statement's applicability, not frequency or agreement; the symmetric five-option scale preserves the source's 1–5 format. Pilot comprehension of “inexacto” in Mexican Spanish before launch.

For each item, accept integers 1–5 only. Positive key: scored value = response. Negative key: scored value = 6 − response. Facet = sum of its four scored values, range 4–20. Finishing requires all 116 personality items and all three attention items answered; no prorating, imputation or partial results. Attention items never enter personality sums. No domain scores, total personality score, rank, percentile or overall fit in v1; Openness has only five facets. Backend computes scores; keys and scoring metadata are not candidate payloads. Presentation order is ascending original number, with attention insertions defined in [quality signals](quality-signals-v1.md).
