# Bands, competencies and job comparison v1

These are semantic summaries of self-description, not population norms, ability estimates or hiring recommendations. Every competency link and arithmetic combination below is a **PeopleFlow hypothesis to explore in the interview**, not a validated scale. Higher is not universally better. No domain scores in v1.

## Fixed semantic bands

Apply to each complete four-item facet sum, after reversing negative items. Boundaries are fixed numbers, not configurable, percentile-derived or sample-adjusted. Band ids are shared throughout the content package.

| Band id | Spanish label | Exact facet sum | Exact general explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| band.low | Bajo | 4–9 inclusive | En conjunto, se describe poco de esta manera. |
| band.medium | Medio | 10–14 inclusive | Se describe de esta manera en parte; conviene precisar en qué situaciones. |
| band.high | Alto | 15–20 inclusive | En conjunto, se describe así con claridad. |

## PeopleFlow competency catalog

Six original competency names. Definitions are interview targets, not claims that IPIP measures these work behaviors directly. The facet links are transparent editorial rationales, with equal weight as a simple reproducible rule; no fitted or validated predictive weights are asserted. Unmapped facets remain available only as self-description; do not silently add them to competencies. N1–N6 and E6 do not drive any competency or job fit in v1.

| Competency id | Spanish name | Definition shown to recruiter | Linked facets and direction | Editorial rationale / evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| comp.organizacion | Orden para avanzar | Organizar recursos y dar seguimiento a un plan hasta cerrar tareas. | C2 positive; C5 positive | Preference for order plus following plans may support task tracking; actual delivery needs examples. Narrow conscientiousness research is background [R2], not validation of this pairing. |
| comp.compromisos | Cuidado de acuerdos | Acordar entregables, cumplir lo acordado y avisar cuando hace falta ajustar. | C3 positive; C4 positive | Keeping commitments plus effort may support agreed delivery; no inference of honesty. [R2] does not establish these weights or this definition. |
| comp.coordinacion | Impulso para coordinar | Proponer un rumbo y acercarse a otras personas para repartir y revisar el trabajo. | E3 positive; E1 positive | Taking charge and approaching others may facilitate coordination; neither establishes effective management. Broad trait–competency work [R1] is background only. |
| comp.colaboracion | Apoyo entre pares | Ofrecer ayuda y resolver diferencias sin romper los acuerdos de trabajo. | A3 positive; A4 positive | Time for others and less confrontation may support assistance; avoiding disagreement can also impede useful challenge. [R1] does not validate this link. |
| comp.exploracion | Apertura a probar | Considerar alternativas y comprender ideas antes de probar cambios en el trabajo. | O4 positive; O5 positive | Variety preference and abstract interest may support exploration, not ability or innovation outcomes. [R1] is background only. |
| comp.deliberacion | Pausa para decidir | Revisar consecuencias y elegir cuánto comprobar antes de actuar. | C6 positive; E5 negative | Reflection and less pursuit of intense experiences may support checking consequences; too much checking can delay action. Editorial hypothesis, not an established scale. [R1, R2] are background only. |

Each link is a hypothesis to explore in the interview. Display this sentence alongside every competency: «La relación con esta competencia es una hipótesis. Confírmala con ejemplos de trabajo.»

### Deterministic competency band

For positive links use the facet sum `s`; for negative links use `24 − s` (range 4–20). Compute the exact arithmetic mean of the two oriented facet sums; retain halves without rounding. This is a **descriptive index**, not a new validated competency test or a domain score.

- `band.low`: index ≥4 and <10.
- `band.medium`: index ≥10 and <15.
- `band.high`: index ≥15 and ≤20.

Thus 9.5 is low, 10 is medium, 14.5 is medium, 15 is high. Facet boundaries above remain integer 4–9 / 10–14 / 15–20. Store the two oriented sums and index with the band for audit. Disagreement between facets can be hidden by a mean: always show contributing facets, and do not claim uniformity. No extra disagreement focus is defined in v1. If any item is missing, compute neither index nor fit.

## Vacancy profile model

One entry per catalog competency, keyed by its stable id; duplicates and unknown ids are invalid. Save a profile version and an explicit recruiter-confirmed job-related rationale before using it in a result. Suggestions from the vacancy are editable drafts, never automatically confirmed or treated as evidence of job validity. A new profile version affects future results only unless an explicit new comparison is recorded; never overwrite a prior comparison.

| Importance id | Recruiter label | Required desired band | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| importance.clave | Clave | Exactly one of the three band ids | The role regularly requires this behavior; recruiter records a concrete task. |
| importance.importante | Importante | Exactly one of the three band ids | Useful for a specified task, but not central to every delivery. |
| importance.no_relevante | No relevante | Null | Not used for fit, focus or question prioritization. |

Default is No relevante, desired band null. Do not default every desired band to high. Low, medium or high may be desired; desired low means a different work style, not an adverse judgment. Example: «Pausa para decidir · Importante · Medio: tomar decisiones reversibles sin detener la operación.» Missing confirmed profile: show bands only, «Define el perfil de la vacante para explorar la alineación.»; fit is null, no gap focus. Absence of a profile never blocks a completed assessment.

## Fit and focus rules

Use ranks low=0, medium=1, high=2 solely for distance `d = abs(observed − desired)`. Store direction separately as below/above desired; high is not automatically aligned. Only relevant competencies with a confirmed profile receive fit.

| Fit id | Recruiter label | Fixed rule |
|---|---|---|
| fit.alineado | Alineado | d = 0 |
| fit.explorar | Por explorar | d = 1, OR d = 2 with importance.importante |
| fit.atencion | Foco de atención | d = 2 with importance.clave |

For No relevante or an unconfirmed profile, fit is null, display «Sin comparación». Quality signals do not change these values. No global fit, numerical match percentage, pass/fail, automatic discard, invitation block or pipeline movement is permitted.

| Focus type id | Creation rule | Exact recruiter text |
|---|---|---|
| focus.adjacent | One per relevant competency with d = 1 | Lo declarado está a una banda de lo que requiere la vacante. Pide un ejemplo para precisar cómo se adapta a esa tarea. |
| focus.distant | One per relevant competency with d = 2, whether Importante or Clave | Lo declarado está a dos bandas de lo que requiere la vacante. Contrasta un ejemplo de esa tarea y los apoyos que usa para realizarla. |
| focus.quality | One per result if tier.algunas or tier.relevantes, independent of gaps or profile | Conviene aclarar cómo fue la experiencia al responder y confirmar lo declarado con ejemplos de trabajo. |

Every gap focus carries competency id, observed band, desired band, importance, fit and profile version. Instance identity is `(result_id, focus_type, competency_id)`; quality uses null competency and carries tier plus triggered signal ids. For guide routing only, attach quality to the highest-importance relevant competency, ties by competency id ascending; if none, attach to comp.organizacion. This is a question context, not evidence of a problem with that competency. Do not duplicate global quality across competencies.

Order gap focuses by Clave before Importante, then d descending, then competency id ascending. Display global quality after gaps; tier.relevantes uses the stronger quality message in the signals document. Focus means a topic to explore, never a discard. Recruiter footer: «Úsalo para preparar la entrevista. La decisión requiere evidencia adicional y revisión humana.»
