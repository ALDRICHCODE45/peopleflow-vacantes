## Specs

- **S1** (L1, L2). «Equipo» shows the company's real members from GET /me/company/members: name, email, role (Dueño / Reclutador) and member since; never prototype members. Loading, error with retry and empty states.
- **S2** (L2). Each member's workload is real: active vacancies where the member is the principal recruiter (`principal_recruiter.id` = member `user_id`, status published or paused) and the candidates in process of those vacancies, from GET /v2/me/company/job-drafts.
- **S3** (L2). An owner can change another member's role (PATCH /me/company/members/{id}, body `{role}`) and remove a member (DELETE, with confirmation). No actions on your own row, and no demote/remove on the only owner (counted from the list). Recruiters see no actions.
- **S4** (L2). The inert «Invitar» card is removed (no invite-by-email backend); noted as a future backend unit.
- **S5** (L1). «Base de talento» becomes «Candidatos de mi organización» v1 on the backend contract (pending, backend/docs/org-candidates.md): list with table and card views over the same results, total count, filters (location, minimum education + in progress, seniority, years ranges, languages + minimum level, vacancy, origin manual/organic, q name/email), detail with contact, harmonized profile, CVs and participations per vacancy. Access: every recruiter of the company sees the whole internal pool.
- **S7** (L10). «cuando ingreso sesion con empresa, me dice eso pero realmente si inice sesion»: a sign-in must not end on «No pudimos completar el acceso» when Cognito accepted it.
- **S8** (L11). Employer area: «Mandar al login (recomendado)» — without a session any `/empresa/...` route redirects to `/empresa/login` and returns to the requested route after signing in; the local bootstrap keeps its own route.
- **S6** (L3). Use shadcn components and their official documentation examples as the reference for each component; check visual consistency (light, dark, mobile), not only function.
